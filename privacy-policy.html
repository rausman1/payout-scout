Payout Scout privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Payout Scout is a browser extension that compares prices for the same bet across prediction-market platforms. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. We never receive your data.

What stays in your browser

  • Settings: the platforms you use, your alert level and cost settings. These use Chrome's sync storage, so if you sync your Chrome profile, Google carries them to your other devices.
  • Your usual stake and whether you've seen the tour: kept on this device only.
  • Your history: up to 5,000 comparisons (the market, the prices and payouts, and any "same bet" marks you add), kept on this device only so the history page can show them. Clear it any time with "Clear history".

What is sent to other sites

When you open a market page on Kalshi, Polymarket, Polymarket US or Novig, or press "Try it" on the welcome page, your browser asks those platforms' public price services for that market and the same market on the platforms you selected. Those requests contain the market being looked up, not your name or account. Like any web request, they show your IP address to the platform that answers.

Payout Scout only runs on those four sites. It doesn't read your balance, your bets or your login, and it never places bets. It remembers the label of the button you last clicked on a market page, for a moment, to tell which bet you're looking at; that label stays in your browser, and appears in a report only if you copy one yourself.

What we don't do

We don't collect, sell or share personal information, and we don't track you across websites.

Reports you choose to send

"Copy report" puts technical details about a scan (the market, prices and settings) on your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you paste it somewhere yourself.

Deleting your data

"Clear history" deletes your history. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored on this device.

Children

Payout Scout is for adults who can legally use these platforms. It isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change too. Contact details are on the extension's store listing.
